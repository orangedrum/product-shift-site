**UserMirror Persona Logic Hub:** Build an intelligence layer within UserMirror that turns community data (WhatsApp, Transcripts) into actionable personas and experiments.

**Continuous Community Intelligence:** Build a robust infrastructure beneath UserMirror that continuously captures user sentiments, builds data-backed personas, and validates product/marketing initiatives through real-world experiments. This infrastructure will underpin and enhance our existing Synthetic UX Testing.

Moving from "Guessing" (Synthetic personas) to "Proving" (Community-backed data).

## 3. Detailed Specifications

### A. The Community Vault (Ingestion Engine)
- **Purpose:** A "Multi-Source Shredder" to ingest and atomize community data.
- **Sources:** WhatsApp exports (including media), meeting/podcast transcripts, manual observation notes, and social media (via `scrapeUrl`).
- **Extraction (AI-driven):** Motivations, Demographics, Engagement, Objections, Triggers, Feature Requests, Connections.
- **Smart De-duplication:** 
    - Content Hashing for raw text.
    - **Smart Delta Ingestion:** Ingest ONLY new data from re-uploaded WhatsApp groups/transcripts based on timestamps.
    - Similarity Scan for insights (allow similar but unique; prevent exact duplicates).
- **Media Handling:** Direct ingestion from WhatsApp + independent uploads + "Pinning" mechanism to sessions.

### B. The Persona Workshop (Persona Management Hub)
- **Interface:** Grid of "Known Personas" + "Build My Own Persona" CTA.
- **User Mirror Flow:** 
    1. Demographics Setup.
    2. Ingestion Sequence (reusing Resume Engine UI).
    3. Persona Synthesis: AI identifies patterns, generates 1 definitive persona, and suggests additional segments with reasoning and vault links.
- **Versioning:** Evolution tracking (Alex v1, Alex v2, etc.) with a "Reasoning Log" explaining shifts based on new Vault evidence.
- **Recalibration:** "Review & Recalibrate" button triggers updates after new data ingestion.

### C. The Experiment Hub (Investor Proof Page)
- **Framework:** Structured real-world experiment tracking (e.g., Tango workshops).
- **Core Logic:** Session-based collection, pre/post sentiment tracking, and loopback (closed experiment findings feed back into the Vault).
- **Investor Proof Page Layout (Refactored PublicResume.tsx):**
    - **Kickstarter Treatment:** Stats for `$ Pledged`, `% Funded`, `Goal`.
    - **Action:** Functional Stripe integration for pledges via "Back This Project".
    - **Tabs:**
        - **Overview:** About, Goals, Budget (Circle chart via Recharts).
        - **Findings:** Chronological feed of "Lab Posts" with text, media, and date stamps.
        - **Discussion:** Donor-only comments (comments are ingested into the Vault).

### D. GHL (GoHighLevel) Flywheel
- **Automation:** Suggested social posts based on community highlights or experiment outcomes.
- **Media:** Webhook payload includes signed URLs for associated photos/videos.
- **Attribution:** "Join our Community" links include `?ref=post_id` for effectiveness tracking.
