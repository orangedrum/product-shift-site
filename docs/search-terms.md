## Strategic takeaways

1. **Don't chase head terms** ("ai in customer service" 8.1K). Vendor-saturated, high competition, low conversion for a new entrant.
2. **Own the niche**: "website design audit ai" (1,150) and "ai website audit" (700) are User Mirror's category. Low volume but high intent, low competition, recurring (monitoring). This is where we can win fast.
3. **Ignore consumer terms**: "ai chatbots" (110K) is consumer intent, not buyer intent. Wasted effort.
4. **Avoid echo chamber**: "designer ux", "ux design what is" attract learners, not buyers.
5. **AX-specific terms are unmeasured**: "AI UX", "AI experience design", "AI agent trust", "conversational AI onboarding" — no volume data yet. These are category-creation terms. Low volume now, but we're building the category. Pull real numbers with a keyword tool before committing content calendar.
6. **Highest CPC = budget signal**: "artificial intelligence in customer service" at $115.62 CPC means buyers have money. Long-tail variants of this (e.g., "how to improve AI chatbot customer service experience") are the opportunity.
